Last week I got invited onto [Kodsnack](https://kodsnack.se/), a Swedish podcast about code, for coders. Fredrik wanted to talk about Elixir. This was a bold move on his part, because I will talk about Elixir for as long as anyone lets me, and a podcast is basically a room where someone has agreed in advance to let me.

We covered how I stumbled into Elixir, what it was like getting it into production for the first time, and why I keep handing it to coworkers like a pamphlet at the airport. Have you heard the good news about the BEAM? Your processes crash, a supervisor restarts them, and everyone moves on with their day. I would love that kind of emotional resilience.

We also got into Phoenix, LiveView, the new type system, and Goatmire, which is a conference where you can talk about Elixir for three days straight and nobody slowly backs away from you.

The episode is called "Not here for the syntax," which is fair. Although I will admit the pipe operator didn't hurt.

Give it a listen: [Kodsnack 722: Not here for the syntax, with Koushik Dasika](https://kodsnack.se/722/#elixir)

If you finish it and suddenly feel the urge to rewrite your backend in Elixir, that's between you and your team lead. I'm just the guy with the pamphlets.

Thanks to Fredrik and the Kodsnack crew for having me.
